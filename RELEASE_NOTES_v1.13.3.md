# Lotly v1.13.3

## Deal Room financial evidence release

- Keeps the locked Discover design and locked Deal Room Snapshot layout.
- Qualifies the price edge as discount to **modelled value**, not established market value.
- Renames seller leverage to **Negotiation leverage** until verified seller evidence exists.
- Makes profit labels explicit: working-price profit and guide-price profit are no longer presented as if they are the same calculation.
- Uses a red readiness progress bar when the deal is BID BLOCKED.
- Rebuilds Financials around four acquisition scenarios: opening offer, guide, mid-ceiling stress point, and maximum bid.
- Shows all-in cost, modelled profit/equity, ROI, margin and headroom to the ceiling for each scenario.
- Adds a plain-English explanation when profit differs between working price and guide price.
- Adds a Comparables defensibility verdict: SUPPORTED, USABLE WITH REVIEW, or NOT YET DEFENSIBLE.
- Adds comparable proof cards, model spread, guide-vs-midpoint context and a top-5 best-match sold-evidence table.
- Keeps valuation language explicitly desktop/acquisition-screening only, not a RICS valuation.
