# Discover Design Lock

The Discover page visual design was approved and locked at the v1.12.10 baseline.

From v1.13.0 onward, changes to Discover should be limited to:
- genuine bugs or broken responsive behaviour;
- data accuracy/correctness;
- explicitly requested product changes.

Do not restyle, re-space, re-theme or restructure Discover as part of unrelated feature work. Deal Room, Pipeline, Reports and other workspaces may evolve independently.
