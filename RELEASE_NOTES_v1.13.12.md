# Lotly v1.13.12 — Beginner Auction Story + Seller Accuracy

## Seller
- A lender/receiver disposal is only described as **confirmed** when the seller/disposal type is backed by a verified legal document.
- Listing-only mortgagee/receiver wording is now presented as a **possible lender-led disposal**, not a fact.
- Replaced the misleading `100% evidence confidence` wording with a plain-English confidence statement about **negotiation signals**.
- Reworked the auctioneer call script into a natural, readable conversation using the clean property address and clear non-binding language.

## Auction
- Rebuilt the Auction tab for inexperienced investors around four questions: **What happened? Where is it now? Has the price moved? What does that mean?**
- Added a plain-English current auction stage, next action and three-step auction playbook.
- Clearly separates an observed failed/post-auction signal from assumptions about seller distress or willingness to discount.
- Keeps legal STOP items in force: a post-auction price test is not treated as permission to bid.
- Replaced the raw-first auction timeline with a beginner-friendly event timeline; raw evidence remains available in an expander.

## Safety / locked areas
- Discover, Snapshot, Financials, Comparables and Legal & Planning layouts remain locked.
