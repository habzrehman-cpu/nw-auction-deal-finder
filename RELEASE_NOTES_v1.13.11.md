# Lotly v1.13.11 - Beginner-first Seller & Negotiation

This release rebuilds the Deal Room Seller tab for buyers with capital but limited property-investment experience.

## What changed

- Replaces raw seller tables at the top of the tab with a plain-English negotiation workspace.
- Separates **observable negotiation signals** from assumptions about the seller's private motivation.
- Shows a simple **Strong / Good / Moderate / Limited** negotiation position with a 10-point leverage score and evidence confidence.
- Adds four beginner decision cards: who is selling, why they may negotiate, suggested opening position, and when to stop.
- A red legal/acquisition blocker now turns the suggested opening offer into **PRICE TEST ONLY - NOT A BID** and withholds the maximum-bid ceiling as permission to proceed.
- Adds a three-step negotiation playbook and a property-specific auctioneer call script.
- Adds four simple questions to ask the auctioneer before moving on price.
- Clearly separates **What Lotly knows** from **What the evidence may mean**.
- Seller identity is only shown as confirmed when authoritative subject-title evidence supports it.
- Existing Companies House, timeline and professional-contact intelligence is retained under advanced evidence sections.

Discover, Snapshot, Financials, Comparables and Legal & Planning remain visually locked.
