# Lotly v1.12.3 — Finished Product Experience

Lotly is designed for a professional property buyer who wants to answer one question immediately: **what deserves my attention today?**

## Brand treatment
- The top-left navigation begins with a teal/navy gradient Lotly brand hero.
- A low-contrast residential roofline sits behind the logo to reinforce the property category without competing with navigation.
- The brand hero is intentionally compact so the working controls remain visible above the fold.

## Above-the-fold hierarchy
1. Lotly navigation and workspace
2. "Find your next opportunity"
3. Today / since-last-visit intelligence
4. Residential or Commercial market mode
5. For You / Post-auction / Reduced / New / Bid Ready
6. Sticky search, auction-house and sort controls
7. Top Opportunity and ranked card feed

## Browse-card standard
Every card should reveal, without opening the Deal Room:
- Property image or branded Lotly placeholder
- Auction house / lot / asset type
- Lotly Score
- Current auction status and the three most material signals
- Guide
- Estimated value
- Max buy
- Potential profit / equity
- Guide discount to estimated value where available
- Why the lot ranks
- Open Deal Room / Shortlist / Quick Look / Compare

## Professional-buyer principles
- **Money first.** Surface the investment case before secondary metadata.
- **Movement matters.** New, reduced and newly post-auction lots are identified from persisted history.
- **Unknown is visible.** Missing legal or planning evidence can never appear as safety.
- **One dominant action.** Open Deal Room is the primary CTA; everything else is secondary.
- **Consistent visual grammar.** Navy = authority, teal = interaction, amber = caution, red = blocker.
- **No dead space.** Missing photography uses a deliberate Lotly-branded placeholder at the exact card ratio.
- **Decision density, not clutter.** Browse cards cap visible badges and move the rest into deeper views.

## Product character
Premium proptech rather than estate-agent software: calm, analytical, decisive and trustworthy. The interface should feel credible to an experienced investor while remaining immediately understandable to a first-time user.
