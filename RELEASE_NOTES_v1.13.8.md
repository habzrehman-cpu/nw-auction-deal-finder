# Lotly v1.13.8 — Authoritative legal evidence + beginner readiness

This release tightens the Legal & Planning evidence rules after testing a real uploaded auction pack.

- **Ownership & title can no longer show CLEAR without the official title register.** Title/owner wording repeated in special conditions or another pack document is treated as useful evidence, but not as a substitute for the Land Registry title register.
- **Lease/tenure can no longer show CLEAR from listing wording alone.** A long lease shown by the auction listing remains a positive signal, but CLEAR requires the lease document itself plus a verified lease-term extraction.
- Added a prominent **Legal readiness %** with plain-English states: NOT READY TO BID, READY FOR PROFESSIONAL CHECK, and READY FOR SOLICITOR SIGN-OFF.
- Added the permanent beginner rule: **Never bid while a red STOP item remains.**
- Critical legal findings are translated into plain English. Rentcharge/arrears warnings now explain the practical buyer risk and the exact question to ask a solicitor.
- Building-safety guidance now explains **EWS1** in plain English.
- Added a short glossary for title register, title plan, special conditions, lease, addendum and EWS1.
- The solicitor checklist now mirrors the property’s actual STOP/CHECK issues, includes missing authoritative documents and critical risk flags, is copy-friendly, and can be downloaded as a checklist.
- Discover, Deal Room Snapshot, Financials, Comparables and the overall Legal & Planning visual layout remain locked.
