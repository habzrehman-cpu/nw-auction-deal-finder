# Lotly v1.13.0 - Deal Room Decision Cockpit

## Product direction
- Discover is now design-locked.
- Deal Room becomes the primary investment decision workspace.

## Deal Room
- New property decision cockpit with image, Lotly score, auction context and live evidence status.
- Guide, estimated value/GDV, opening offer, maximum buy and profit/equity shown together.
- Clear PURSUE/WATCH/PASS decision state and next recommended move.
- Evidence strip for comparables, legal pack, planning and underwriting confidence.
- Deal brief download available directly from the header.
- Snapshot reorganised around investment case, risks, readiness and prioritised next actions.
- Existing Seller, Financials, Comparables, Auction, Legal & Planning, Location and Workspace functionality retained.

## Deal Room index
- Command-centre KPIs for priority opportunities, active rooms, decision readiness and post-auction leverage.
- Two-column priority room layout with guide, maximum buy, readiness, stage and Lotly score.

## Operations
- Sidebar version label now reads the VERSION file dynamically.
