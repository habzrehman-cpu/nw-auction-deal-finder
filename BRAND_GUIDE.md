# Lotly Brand Guide

## Brand
**Lotly** is a property-auction intelligence product designed to help professional buyers find, assess and act on auction opportunities faster.

**Descriptor:** Property Auction Intelligence  
**Product promise:** Find your next opportunity.

## Visual identity
- Deep navy: `#0B1F33` — primary typography and authority
- Lotly teal: `#0F8F83` — primary actions and selected states
- Mint: `#2DD4BF` — secondary accents and positive signals
- Soft background: `#F7FAFB`
- Risk red: reserved for genuine blockers and material risk only
- Amber: reserved for caution, incomplete evidence and watch states

## UX principles
1. **Answer first, evidence second.** Show the buying decision before the underlying detail.
2. **Visual scanning matters.** Property imagery, price and deal score should be understood in seconds.
3. **Progressive disclosure.** Keep technical due diligence one click deeper unless it is a blocker.
4. **Evidence confidence is visible.** Unknown is never treated as safe.
5. **One primary action per context.** Avoid forcing the user to interpret a wall of controls.
6. **Professional, not gimmicky.** Motion and colour should create confidence, not distraction.

## Logo use
Use the full Lotly wordmark in desktop headers, reports and marketing. Use the icon-only mark for app favicons, compact navigation and future mobile use. Preserve clear space around the mark and do not recolour it outside the approved navy/teal palette.

## v1.12 interaction system
- **Navigation:** Discover, Shortlist, Pipeline and Settings. Buying criteria no longer dominate the sourcing screen.
- **Primary discovery:** Top Opportunity spotlight followed by a two-column visual feed.
- **Brand score:** "Lotly Score" is the primary ranking language throughout the customer experience.
- **Selected state:** Lotly teal only. Red is reserved for material risk and bid blockers.
- **Progressive disclosure:** Quick Look -> Deal Room -> underlying evidence.
- **Refresh model:** data updating is a utility action, not the main call-to-action.
- **Comparison:** up to four lots can be compared side by side without leaving discovery.

## v1.12.1 presentation standard
- Use the Lotly mark once per screen hierarchy; avoid duplicate logo/masthead treatment.
- Fixed image ratios are part of the brand rhythm. Missing imagery must use the branded Lotly placeholder rather than empty space.
- "Estimated value" is the customer-facing term; methodology and desktop valuation language belongs in the evidence layer.
- The browse experience must show price movement and guide-to-value spread without implying a regulated valuation.
- Selected states and interaction use teal. Risk red never indicates ordinary selection.
